About me

Hello, I'm Christine

I'm an accredited counsellor and psychotherapist working from Dominick Street in Galway City. My practice is a space where you can slow down, say what is really going on and be heard without judgement.

I draw on a holistic, integrative way of working, bringing together talking therapy with mindfulness and meditation, so that we look after your mind, your body and the life you are living.
